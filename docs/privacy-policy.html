Privacy Policy — What Am I Connected To?

Last updated: October 7, 2026

"What Am I Connected To?" ("the app") is a network diagnostics tool for Android. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

Network requests

To perform its diagnostic checks, the app makes the following network calls directly from your device:

  • A request to api.ipify.org to determine your current public IP address.
  • A connectivity/ping check to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare's public DNS resolver) to verify that the internet is reachable.

These requests are made only when you open the app or tap "Refresh." No data from these requests is sent anywhere other than directly between your device and the service being checked. The app itself does not log, store, or share the results outside of your device.

Permissions used

  • INTERNET — required to perform the connectivity checks described above.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required to read basic connection info (e.g. gateway, DNS servers) from the Android OS.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — required to read Wi-Fi signal strength.

None of these permissions are used to identify you personally or to track your activity.

Sharing results

The app lets you manually share your connection report (e.g. via a messaging app or email) using Android's built-in share feature. This only happens when you explicitly tap "Share result" — the app never shares data on its own.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
